02 · Idea

Pick an idea that stands out.

When everyone can build with AI, the idea matters most.

Shout it out

Give me a tourism app idea.

The first ideas AI gives you
AI trip plannerChatbot tour guideAR heritage app"Hidden gems" mapBooking website

Expect at least 5 teams to build one of these.

Don't build the first idea AI gives you.

Generic
"An AI travel planner for Sarawak."
Specific and local
A homestay host sends one voice note on WhatsApp. AI turns it into a page tourists can book.

Make it specific and local. A bit weird is fine.

Explain your problem
in 10 seconds.

If you can't, pick a simpler problem.

One of your judges already told you
"Most kampung sellers live on WhatsApp and a phone."
"A small prototype that runs beats a big idea on slides."
Chuo Nang Ling, Founder, Sarawak Fruit Enterprise · this morning

Research your judges' backgrounds.

Technical

"Does it fit real work?"
